# Spooldesk funding options

Status checked 15 October 2026. The strongest immediate path is customer validation followed by narrowly scoped paid implementation/support. Grant eligibility is not established by adding an AI API integration. No applications have been submitted and no funding is committed.

The [USF general applicant FAQ](https://usf.com.ua/contacts/) requires a Ukrainian registered legal entity or FOP for the grant programs it describes. Each new call governs eligibility; do not register or incur costs based only on an old program summary. EIC also describes paths for individuals intending to establish an SME, so the USF rule must not be generalized to all programs.

## Evidence to prepare before applying

1. A one-page explanation of the buyer, problem and why self-hosting matters.
2. A working pinned installer and a two-minute demo with no customer data.
3. Three pilot outcomes, buyer objections and evidence of willingness to pay. These are milestones to reach, not current traction claims.
4. An honest comparison with established helpdesks: installation effort, channel workflow, operational control and measurable AI quality. Do not claim proprietary model training when documents are passed as context.
5. A six-month milestone budget covering release reliability, onboarding, security review and acquisition. Use supplier quotes or explicit assumptions rather than a guessed funding total.
6. Confirmed ownership/licensing, applicant legal status, banking/payment readiness and program-specific declarations. Keep private documents out of the repository.

## Recommended order

Run the [first customer campaign](MARKETING_LAUNCH.md), collect evidence, then decide whether the next Startup EDGE call fits. Seek paid pilot/implementation revenue only after contractual and tax readiness is resolved. Treat infrastructure credits as cost reduction, not revenue or a reason to convert the product to SaaS. Defer an equity round until a repeatable buyer need and credible growth model are visible.

Limit initial grant research to a small weekly time budget. Preparing a lengthy application for a closed call or a poor technical fit would delay the most valuable evidence: a team installing and using Spooldesk.
